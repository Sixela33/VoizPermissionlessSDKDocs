---
title: "Pause trading"
description: "Creator one-way pause. Buy, sell, and addFunding stop."
---

The creator can pause once (no unpause). `buy` / `sell` / `addFunding` stop. `removeFunding` and redeem stay open.

```ts
await market.pauseTrading({ owner })
```
