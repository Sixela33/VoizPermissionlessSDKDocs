---
title: "Pause trading"
description: "Freeze trading before the result. One-way; existing liquidity can still leave."
---

You are the creator. You need to freeze trading on the ETH question before the result is known. Pause is one-way. It is not the result, and you cannot undo it. Buys, sells, and new liquidity stop. Existing liquidity can still be withdrawn. Redeem stays open.

```ts
await market.pauseTrading({ owner })
```
