title: "Remove liquidity"
description: "Take inventory out while the market is still open."
---

You are taking inventory out while the ETH market is still open (including paused). You burn pool shares and receive a basket of every answer, including Invalid — not cash. Then [`merge`](/market/merge) complete sets if you want collateral. After the result is published, cash the pool shares with [`redeemLp`](/market/redeem-lp) instead.

```ts
await market.removeFunding({ shares, owner })
```

Find the lot with [`user.lpPositions`](/user/get-lp-positions). Send-back / leftover named tokens are share positions — cash those with [`market.redeem`](/market/redeem).
