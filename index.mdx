VOIZ turns a question into a live market. This SDK is how you talk to that market: one client, then a market, a pool, or a user. Viem is underneath. You do not assemble calldata.

```ts
client.listMarkets()
client.market(address)
client.pool(outcomeToken, { market, outcomeIndex })
client.user(owner)
```

A **market** is the protocol object. **Metadata** (description, resolution rules, images) is a separate read — [how to think about it](/concepts). Each market has one OutcomeAmm underneath — [how VOIZ works](/protocol).
