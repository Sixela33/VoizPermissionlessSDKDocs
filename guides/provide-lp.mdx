---
title: "Provide liquidity"
description: "One collateral amount into the market's OutcomeAmm."
---

You put in `credits`. The SDK calls `OutcomeAmm.addFunding` on this market's AMM. That is `market.addLiquidity` after the book is open.

First LP is `market.seed({ seedWei, oddsBps, owner })` — same contract, with a distribution hint from the odds (Invalid weight 0).

For inventory, fees, and resolution, read [How liquidity provision works](/guides/liquidity) first.

## You need

A seeded market (`seedStatus()` is `"seeded"` when AMM `totalSupply > 0`), enough collateral, and `owner` as the connected wallet.

```ts
const market = client.market(marketAddress)
const credits = parseUnits('50', 18)

await market.addLiquidity({
  credits,
  owner: yourAddress,
  setProgress, // SDK sends "Minting shares…"
})
```

Later LP uses an empty distribution hint and joins **current** proportions. Tiny `credits` fail with “Amount too small…”.

You receive ERC-20 pool shares (`vAMM`), one lot per market. Find them with `client.user(addr).lpPositions({ market })`.

Exit open-market with `market.removeFunding({ shares, owner })` (token basket). After resolve, `market.redeemLp({ shares, owner })` pays collateral. Creator can `market.pauseTrading()` once (no unpause).
